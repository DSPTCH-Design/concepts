What to ask before your first aesthetic consultation

A useful starting point: discuss qualifications, your goals, realistic expectations, risks, alternatives, costs and aftercare.

A first consultation works best as a two-way conversation. These questions help you leave with a clear picture:

  • Who will perform the treatment, and what is their training?
  • What results are realistic for me, and how long might they last?
  • What are the risks, and how are complications handled?
  • Are there alternatives, including doing nothing for now?
  • What does the full cost look like, including follow-up?
  • What should I do before and after?

The value of a personalized treatment plan

Why an individual assessment matters more than following the latest aesthetic trend.

Trends move quickly. Faces, skin and priorities do not follow a calendar. A personalized plan starts with how you look and feel today, your history, and what you want to keep as much as what you want to change.

It also leaves room to pace decisions over time, so each step can be reviewed before the next.

Skin

Understanding skin treatments: where to begin

An introduction to the questions that help distinguish skin concerns, treatment options and routine care.

Start by naming the concern in your own words: texture, dullness, uneven tone or something else. Then separate what daily care can support from what may call for professional treatment.

A skin assessment helps match options to your skin type and lifestyle, including the recovery time you can realistically allow.
