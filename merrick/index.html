Our services

Every kind of roof.
One careful crew.

From a few lifted shingles to a full tear-off, the same Merrick crew handles the job from the first inspection to the final magnet sweep of your yard.

01

Roof Replacement

Full tear-off and re-roof with architectural shingles, new underlayment, ice-and-water shield and flashing, usually done in one to three days.

02

Roof Repair

Leaks, missing shingles, failed flashing and rotted decking found, fixed and photographed so you can see exactly what we did.

03

Flat & Commercial Roofing

Modified bitumen, TPO and EPDM systems for two-flats, garages, storefronts and low-slope commercial buildings.

04

Storm & Hail Damage

Emergency tarping around the clock, a documented damage report, and someone on the roof with your adjuster.

05

Roof Inspections

A free 21-point inspection with photos and a plain-language report. If the roof has years left, we'll tell you.

06

New Roof Installation

Roofs for additions, garages and new builds, specified for snow load, ventilation and long Midwest winters.
