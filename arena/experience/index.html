Healthcare,without thewaiting room.

We built ARENA around the calendar of someone who doesn't have time to be sick, and doesn't intend to be.

Designed around your week, not ours.

Your physician, directly.

Message the doctor who knows your case. Not a call center, not a rotating roster.

A care lead for the logistics.

Scheduling, lab orders, prescriptions, referrals. One person, one thread.

Same-week appointments.

In clinic or by video, built around your week rather than ours.

Labs where you are.

A phlebotomist comes to your home or office, often before your first meeting of the day.

A private clinic.

One member at a time. No waiting room, no clipboard, no fluorescent hallway.

On the road, too.

Video visits where licensing allows, and lab access across the country for members who travel.

The appointment starts on time. That shouldn't be a selling point. It is.

From first call to first plan.

Most members have their written plan in hand within three weeks of the intro call.

DAY 1

Intro call with your care lead. Intake forms, history, goals.

DAY 3–5

Labs drawn at your home or office.

DAY 7–10

90-minute assessment with your physician: DEXA, VO2 max, strength, conversation.

DAY 14–17

Your written plan, walked through in a follow-up visit.

DAY 30

First check-in. Early adjustments, questions answered.

Discretion is part of the service.

Your information is protected under HIPAA and shared only with the people you authorize. We don't sell data, and we don't market to your contacts. Many members simply prefer that their health stays their business. We're built for that.
