Before you call.

Do you accept insurance?+

ARENA is a membership-based practice and does not bill insurance directly. Some services may be eligible for HSA or FSA funds; check with your plan administrator.

Am I eligible for GLP-1 medication?+

That's a decision your physician makes after an evaluation. We'll be direct with you either way.

Do I need testosterone therapy?+

Maybe, maybe not. We evaluate the full picture with repeat testing and only recommend treatment when it's clinically indicated.

Do you offer peptides?+

Your physician can discuss the evidence for specific peptides. Many are investigational or not FDA-approved, and we only consider treatments that can be legally prescribed.

Do I have to live near the clinic?+

Your assessment is in person. Many follow-ups can happen by video, depending on the state you live in.
