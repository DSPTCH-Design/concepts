About Merrick Roofing

Merrick Roofing was established in 2003 with one truck and a ladder on Oakton Street in Skokie, Illinois. Since that time our company has grown to become one of the most trusted roofing contractors on the North Shore, and we have completed over 3,400 roofs for homeowners and businesses throughout the area. Even though we have grown over the years, we are still a family owned and operated local company and we still believe in treating every customer like a member of our own family.

Our mission is to provide our customers with the highest quality roofing services at fair and competitive prices. We believe that a job worth doing is worth doing right the first time. All of our work is performed by our own crews and we never use subcontractors. Our crews are OSHA trained and fully insured for your protection, and we always clean up the job site when the work is completed.

Our Commitment To You

When you choose Merrick Roofing you can be confident that you are getting a company that stands behind its work. We provide free written estimates on all jobs, and the price on your estimate is the price that you pay. All of our work is backed by our 10 year workmanship warranty in addition to the manufacturer's warranty on materials.

Licensed, Bonded & Insured

Merrick Roofing is fully licensed, bonded and insured in the State of Illinois. Proof of insurance is available upon request. If you have any questions about our company please do not hesitate to contact us.
