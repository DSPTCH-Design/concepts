Welcome to Merrick Roofing

Thank you for visiting the website of Merrick Roofing. We are a family owned and operated roofing company located in Skokie, Illinois and we have been proudly serving Skokie, Evanston, Glenview and the surrounding North Shore communities since 2003. Our company was founded on the principles of honesty, quality workmanship and customer satisfaction, and those are the same principles that we continue to operate by today. Whether you are a homeowner, a landlord, a property manager or a business owner, we have the knowledge and the experience to take care of all of your roofing needs.

At Merrick Roofing we offer a complete range of roofing services including roof replacement, roof repair, flat and commercial roofing, storm and hail damage repair, roof inspections, new roof installation and much more. We are fully licensed, bonded and insured in the State of Illinois and all of our work is performed by our own experienced crews. We use only quality materials from leading manufacturers and all of our work is backed by a 10 year workmanship warranty. Customer satisfaction is our number one priority and we strive to exceed your expectations on each and every job that we do, no matter how big or how small.

We understand that roofing problems can happen at any time, which is why we also offer emergency storm service 24 hours a day. We can also assist you with your insurance claim. Please take a few moments to browse through our website to learn more about our company and the services that we offer. If you have any questions or would like to request a free estimate, please visit our Contact Us page. We look forward to hearing from you!
