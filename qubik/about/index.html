How we work

Three rules
1[room]

One table

Architects and engineers start every project together, in the same room, on the same model. Nobody inherits a drawing they didn't help make.

1[model]

Draw it once

Structure, systems and skin live in one model from schematic design on, so clashes get resolved on screen instead of on site.

0[hand-offs]

Stay on site

The engineer who sized the beam is the one who walks the job. Questions get answered by the person who knows the answer.
