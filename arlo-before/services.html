Our Plumbing Services

Arlo Plumbing is pleased to offer a full range of residential and commercial plumbing services to our customers in Chicago and the surrounding areas. Below you will find more information on some of the services that we provide. If you do not see the service that you are looking for, please contact us as we most likely offer it.

Leak Detection

A hidden water leak can cause a great deal of damage to your home or business if it is not found and repaired in a timely manner. Some of the signs of a hidden leak include an unusually high water bill, damp or discolored spots on walls and ceilings, musty odors and the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use. Our plumbers utilize acoustic and thermal leak detection equipment in order to locate the source of the leak with minimal disruption to your property.

Drain & Sewer Cleaning

Clogged and slow drains are one of the most common plumbing problems that property owners experience. Arlo Plumbing offers complete drain and sewer cleaning services for kitchen sinks, bathroom sinks, tubs, showers, toilets, floor drains and main sewer lines. We offer hydro-jetting as well as video camera inspection of your sewer line, which allows us to identify tree roots, breaks and other problems that are common in older sewer lines.

Water Heater Service

We service, repair and install all major makes and models of water heaters, including conventional tank water heaters and tankless water heaters. If your water heater is leaking, making noise, not producing enough hot water or not producing any hot water at all, give us a call. In many cases we are able to repair or replace your water heater on the same day.

Pipe Repair & Repiping

Many of the older homes and buildings in the Chicago area still have galvanized steel or lead pipes, which can corrode over time and lead to low water pressure, discolored water and leaks. We repair and replace all types of piping including galvanized, lead and copper, and we offer complete repiping services. All of our work is done according to code.

Bath & Kitchen Plumbing

Whether you need a new faucet installed or you are planning a complete bathroom or kitchen remodel, Arlo Plumbing can help. We install and repair faucets, sinks, toilets, tubs, showers, garbage disposals, dishwashers and more. We are also happy to work together with your general contractor on rough-in plumbing for remodeling projects.

Emergency Plumbing

Plumbing emergencies such as burst pipes, sewer backups and overflowing toilets require immediate attention. Arlo Plumbing offers emergency plumbing service 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. Our trucks are stocked with the parts and equipment needed to handle most emergency repairs.

For more information on any of our services, or to request a free estimate, please visit our Contact Us page.
